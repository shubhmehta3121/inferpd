P95 stress — why OOM at high QPS P95 is not plotted here. Under heavy QPS, many long-context sequences ran together; GPU KV filled (max_num_seqs, max_num_batched_tokens, gpu_memory_utilization) and a forward still needed extra workspace → CUDA OOM, engine death, timeouts on the client.

Fix at a high level: limit concurrency, tighten those vLLM caps, optionally free a little VRAM headroom (slightly lower gpu_memory_utilization), scale decoders for more sustained rps.
